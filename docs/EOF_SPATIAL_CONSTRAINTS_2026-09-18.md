# End-of-flight spatial constraints

Official ATSB First Principles: 99 percent of the DST Group analysis results lie within 25 NM east and west of the seventh arc after revised end-of-flight treatment. The remaining prospective corridor is approximately 32.5 to 36 S along the arc.

ATSB manufacturer simulations with descent rates consistent with final BFO evidence generally reached the ocean within 15 NM of the arc. The 15 NM result is simulation evidence, not a hard boundary. Some simulations remained airborne about 20 minutes after second-engine flameout and some travelled rearwards about 21 NM.

The right outboard flap was assessed as probably housed when it separated, reducing support for scenarios involving flap deployment.

Model rule: keep uncontrolled descent, glide, and continued-control branches separate. Do not infer a Gaussian distribution from a 99-percent-within-25-NM containment statement. Do not apply another EOF likelihood to a DSTG final-location product that already contains EOF modelling.
