# Evidence gates before issuing MH370 candidate polygons

A candidate residual search polygon MUST NOT be labelled robust until all gates below pass.

## Gate 1 — Geometry
Use authoritative 7th-arc geometry. Domain for the baseline end-of-flight model is approximately 33S–36S and no farther than the ATSB practical envelope (~27.5 NM west / 25 NM east). Wider glide envelopes are sensitivity cases only.

## Gate 2 — Physics
Use DSTG probability density / trajectory-family information where reproducible. A Gaussian latitude proxy is explicitly preliminary and cannot support a final coordinate.

## Gate 3 — Drift
Replace the broad CSIRO-inspired latitude proxy with a digitised published likelihood or a reproducible particle/drift model. Do not center a final posterior on the published CSIRO maximum by construction.

## Gate 4 — End of flight
Keep uncontrolled descent, partial control and controlled glide as separate kernels. Report sensitivity to scenario weights.

## Gate 5 — Search negative evidence
Intersect cells with authoritative sonar/search geometry. Model non-detection as 1-P_D. LPD, shadow, terrain avoidance and data holidays receive lower P_D than high-quality coverage. Unknown Ocean Infinity geometry receives NO invented spatial penalty.

## Gate 6 — Ablation
Re-run without drift, without EOF, and under conservative/central/strong P_D assumptions. A proposed box must not exist solely because of one arbitrary proxy.

## Gate 7 — Falsification
For every surviving polygon, actively test whether it was previously searched at sufficient resolution, whether debris drift contradicts it, and whether SATCOM/aircraft performance contradict it.

## Current status
NOT PASSED. Public evidence supports concentrating the computational domain near the 7th arc between roughly 33S and 36S, but the repository does not yet contain authoritative cell-level sonar coverage for the entire target corridor nor a reconstructed DSTG density. Therefore no new wreckage coordinate is defensible.
