# OI 2025–26 AUV mission evidence — 2026-09-18

## What is now supported

Public contemporaneous reporting of Ocean Infinity updates describes actual Hugin AUV mission operations, not merely vessel movement.

For the 31 Dec 2025 start, two Hugin AUVs were reported deployed on survey missions in the southern sector. Reporting of 2–4 Jan describes planned missions, routine vessel-to-AUV handshakes, recovery for mission completion, and a third AUV technical fault. Reporting for 6–15 Jan describes up to three Hugin AUVs repeatedly deployed, monitored and recovered for data download/battery replacement.

A separate vessel-configuration analysis reports Armada 78-06 carrying two containerised AUV garages; the 2026 campaign is reported with three Hugin AUVs.

## Evidentiary consequence

This strengthens the distinction already encoded in the project:

surface-vessel AIS track != AUV mission trajectory != sonar swath.

The vessel's zig-zag/loiter pattern can identify mission launch/recovery neighbourhoods, but cannot establish the underwater line followed by an AUV or the width/quality of its sonar coverage.

## What remains missing

No public source recovered in this pass exposes:
- AUV mission coordinate sequences;
- mission-plan polygons;
- per-AUV swath width;
- processed sonar footprint GIS;
- cell-level confidence / Pd.

Therefore OI 2025–26 remains spatially UNALLOCATED in the baseline negative-evidence model.

## Search direction inferred from public activity — proxy only

Public tracking sources consistently describe the campaign as working the southern side / southeast-outside side of the 7th arc and progressing along the arc. This can guide requests for mission files and can be shown as a qualitative activity overlay.

It must not be converted into a searched polygon.

## Falsification rule

Any future claimed OI coverage polygon must be rejected unless its provenance can be traced to:
1. OI/MOT/AUV mission output; or
2. a reproducible transformation of authoritative AUV trajectories plus documented sensor geometry.

A polygon reconstructed solely from AIS/vessel KML fails the gate.
