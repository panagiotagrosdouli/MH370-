# Ocean Infinity track evidence gate — 2026-09-18

## New retrieval result

A current public tracking archive explicitly offers downloadable Google Earth KML/KMZ histories for three 2025–26 legs:

- Armada 78-06: 2025-02-08 to 2025-03-06
- Armada 78-06: 2025-03-06 to 2025-04-08
- Armada 86-05: 2025-12-30 to 2026-01-23

The same archive explicitly states that the precise coordinates of Ocean Infinity's white search bands are not known and that those bands were drawn from a public presentation.

Therefore the downloadable vessel histories are stronger than a hand-drawn band for reconstructing **surface-vessel activity**, but still do not identify AUV seabed swaths.

## Independent technical support for the distinction

A contemporary technical account of Armada 78-06 reports that it was configured with two containerised AUV garages. A separate contemporary update reports three AUVs launched during a five-day February operation.

This supports the project's fail-closed rule: surface vessel track and AUV seabed coverage are different observables.

## Baseline evidence policy

The baseline posterior must continue to assign:
- vessel-track KML: zero search-negative weight;
- presentation-derived white bands: zero search-negative weight;
- official MOT 7,571 km2 total: aggregate accounting only;
- authoritative AUV/sonar footprint: eligible for Pd only after provenance/semantics validation.

## Proxy use that is allowed

Once downloaded, vessel KML may be intersected with the official 33–36S / cross-arc domain only to answer:
- where did the support vessel spend time?
- which parts of the residual domain had proximate search activity?
- where should we prioritize requests for AUV mission files?

It must not answer:
- which cells were searched;
- which cells were excluded;
- current residual posterior after OI.

## Current blocker

The next decisive data object is no longer 'a map of the 2025 search'. It is one of:
1. AUV mission track/mission-plan files;
2. processed sonar coverage footprints;
3. a public GIS export from OI/MOT with search swaths;
4. enough mission metadata to reconstruct swaths with documented sensor geometry.

Until one is recovered, OI spatial non-detection remains UNALLOCATED.
