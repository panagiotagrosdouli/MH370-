01 / PURPOSE

239 people. One unresolved question.

Malaysia Airlines flight MH370 disappeared on 8 March 2014. This independent project aims to make assumptions visible, compare evidence, and quantify uncertainty — with respect for passengers, crew, and their families.

02 / BAYESIAN FRAMEWORK

A probability map, not a prediction.

The research seeks a posterior distribution over possible locations, updating physically plausible areas with compatible observations and evidence from unsuccessful searches.

P(L | D) ∝ Pphysics(L) × ℒdrift(L) × ℒEOF(L) × ℒsearch(L)

Schematic factorization only: shared evidence and dependent likelihoods must not be double-counted.

03 / WHAT THE RESEARCH FOUND

Findings, evidence and limitations.

This page presents findings documented in the open research repository, not a claim that the aircraft or its main wreckage has been located. The strongest defensible output is currently an evidence-constrained search region, not a verified impact coordinate.

MODEL REFERENCE POINT • NOT A DISCOVERY

35.6°S, 92.8°E

Decimal coordinates: -35.6, 92.8. This location appears as the mode of an explicitly simplified, CSIRO-anchored proxy model. The coordinate was an input anchor drawn from published drift-model work; it is not an independently inferred precise impact position. 01 / SATCOM + DRIFT

Priority latitude corridor

Official analyses and drift evidence support investigating the southern Indian Ocean, broadly around 33°S–36°S near the seventh arc. This is a region, not a pinpoint.

02 / END-OF-FLIGHT

Close to the arc

The research baseline prioritizes a corridor of roughly ±25 nautical miles from the arc; longer controlled-glide scenarios are retained as sensitivity cases rather than assumed outcomes.

03 / PREVIOUS SEARCHES

Non-detection is informative

Historical sonar coverage was substantial, but confidence varied due to complex terrain, sonar shadows, data gaps and low-probability-of-detection zones. Survey area totals alone cannot identify specific remaining gaps.

04 / GEOSPATIAL DATA

Verified data sources

The repository identifies official 7th Arc ArcGIS geometry and Geoscience Australia Phase-2 sonar datasets, including the raw/processed data archive (DOI: 10.4225/25/5B0CD2B84A2DC).

05 / SEARCH HISTORY

Later surveys matter

The research log records a 2025–26 Ocean Infinity survey summary but lacks publicly usable cell-level search tracks. No inferred negative-evidence mask is applied to unspecified locations.

06 / MODEL TESTING

Proxy sensitivity only

Exploratory glide-mixture tests showed that varying assumptions can shift residual probability. These are tests of model sensitivity—not validated probabilities of wreckage location.

What remains unresolved

The full official sonar footprints, data holidays and cell-level detection probabilities must be joined to a geodesically accurate search grid; the published DSTG likelihood also requires careful reconstruction. The repository explicitly blocks final cell ranking until its evidence gates are satisfied. A physical location would require independent sonar, ROV or recovery confirmation.

03 / RESEARCH STATUS

What the repository can — and cannot — establish.

Existing

Proxy posterior grids, geodesic utilities, SATCOM checks, drift sensitivities and residual-search methods.

Open gap

Scientific boundary

No confirmed wreckage location

Proxy-mode coordinates are model artifacts, not a discovery. Independent validation is essential.
